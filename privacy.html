1. About this policy

Breathe Strong is a structured exercise, breathing and lifestyle program designed to support men seeking to improve snoring, sleep health and general physical fitness.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, used and managed when you visit the Breathe Strong website or use the Breathe Strong tester application.

We recognise that information about your sleep, health and medical treatment is sensitive. We aim to provide clear choices about how that information is stored and controlled.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Breathe Strong may process information including:

Account information

  • Google account identifier and profile information supplied through Google sign-in, such as your email address, where applicable.
  • Authentication information required to maintain your account.

Health and sleep information

  • Self-reported snoring scores and partner ratings.
  • Daytime tiredness ratings and notes about your night.
  • CPAP usage, events-per-hour readings and mask seal information.
  • Sleep study results entered by you.
  • Information entered from wearable devices.
  • Body measurements and fitness progress.
  • Health-related check answers, appointments and questions.

Exercise and program information

  • Completed exercises and training sessions.
  • Exercise weights, repetitions and progression.
  • Breathing exercise completion.
  • Program preferences and progress history.

Technical information

The website is hosted through Cloudflare and uses externally hosted fonts. The tester application is currently served from GitHub Pages. These providers may receive standard technical information associated with requests, including IP addresses.

Google and Firebase may process technical and account information when you choose cloud sign-in and storage, according to their applicable service arrangements.

We do not require you to provide every optional health metric to use the program.

3. How your information is stored

Breathe Strong currently offers two storage options.

Option A: phone-only storage

  • Your program records are stored locally in your browser on that device.
  • You do not need a Google account.
  • Your logs are not intentionally uploaded to the Breathe Strong cloud database.
  • Clearing browser storage, changing devices or losing your phone may result in data loss.
  • You are responsible for maintaining any backups you choose to create.

Option B: Google sign-in and cloud storage

  • Authentication is provided through Google.
  • Your program records are stored in a Firebase database associated with your account.
  • Records may be synchronised between supported devices.
  • A local copy may also be maintained on your device.
  • Data processing may involve infrastructure outside your country of residence.

4. How we use your information

Information is used to provide the functionality of Breathe Strong, including:

  • Displaying your exercise program.
  • Recording training completion.
  • Showing progress over time.
  • Maintaining your saved preferences.
  • Preparing summaries you can share with your healthcare professional.
  • Synchronising your information when cloud storage is enabled.
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of the service.

We do not use your self-entered health records to diagnose medical conditions.

Breathe Strong does not independently verify the medical accuracy of the information you enter.

5. Your choices and consent

  • You can choose whether to use phone-only storage or Google sign-in.
  • Choosing cloud storage involves processing account and program information through the relevant Google and Firebase services. The tester asks for your consent before you sign in.
  • Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent before collecting or processing sensitive health information.
  • You can choose which optional health and fitness information to enter.
  • You should not enter information about another person without appropriate permission.

6. Sharing and disclosure

  • Breathe Strong does not sell your health records to advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your information may be processed by service providers needed to operate features you choose, including Google authentication and Firebase storage.
  • We do not automatically share your health records with your GP, sleep specialist, employer or insurer.
  • You control whether to export or provide your clinician summary to another person.
  • Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Data security

We take the protection of personal and health information seriously. The service uses the storage and authentication facilities described in this policy.

However, no online system or local device can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect access to your device and Google account.

9. Accessing, exporting and deleting your information

The tester provides controls for managing your records.

  • Export: use the application’s backup feature (Settings, then “Back up to a file”) to save a copy of your data to a file.
  • Phone-only deletion: use Settings, then “Reset all data”.
  • Cloud account deletion: use Settings, then “Delete my account data”.

10. Third-party services

The current service uses or may interact with:

  • Cloudflare for website hosting.
  • GitHub Pages, which currently serves the tester application.
  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • Google sign-in for optional account authentication.
  • Google Firebase for optional cloud storage.
  • External websites linked for purchasing respiratory training equipment.

These services may operate under their own privacy policies. Breathe Strong does not control the privacy practices of unrelated third-party websites.

The application does not currently provide automatic CPAP, wearable or snoring-recorder integrations.

13. Children’s privacy

Breathe Strong is designed for adults, primarily men aged 40 and over. It is not intended for use by children.

If we become aware that a child has supplied personal information without any required consent, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

14. Medical information

Breathe Strong is an exercise, lifestyle and progress-tracking program. It is not a substitute for professional medical assessment, diagnosis or prescribed treatment.

Your personal health records remain information you have supplied, not independently verified clinical findings.

Do not stop or modify CPAP or other prescribed treatment without appropriate clinical advice. See the medical disclaimer.
