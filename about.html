Privacy and your data

At a glance

  • This website has no forms, analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. It is hosted through Cloudflare and loads its fonts from Google Fonts.
  • In the tester app, you choose where your logs live: on your phone only, with no account, or in the cloud with Google sign-in.
  • Your logs contain only what you enter. Much of it is health information, so choose the option you are comfortable with.
  • You can back up or delete your data yourself at any time from the app’s Settings.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy, which is still being finalised.

Back up and delete your data

  • Back up: in the app, open Settings and choose “Back up to a file”. You can save the file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive and restore from it later.
  • Delete, phone-only mode: open Settings and choose “Reset all data”.
  • Delete, Google sign-in: open Settings and choose “Delete my account data”. This permanently deletes your data from the cloud and from the phone.
  • Switching modes: if you move from Google sign-in to phone-only, the cloud copy stays until you delete it.

Medical disclaimer

What Breathe Strong is

  • An exercise, lifestyle and tracking program. It is not a diagnosis or a medical treatment, and it does not claim any regulatory approval or clinical endorsement. Its regulatory classification has not been formally assessed.
  • The program as a whole has not been tested in a clinical trial. The research it draws on is listed under Evidence and safety. Results vary and no outcome is guaranteed.

Your treatment

  • It does not replace your clinician, a sleep study, CPAP, an oral appliance or any other prescribed treatment. Keep using prescribed treatment exactly as directed.
  • Changes to CPAP or other treatment should only be made by your clinician, following reassessment and appropriate objective testing.
  • Snore scores, CPAP machine readings, wearable data and how you feel are useful for spotting trends. They cannot establish that obstructive sleep apnoea has improved or resolved.
  • The checks in the app are for your own information. They are not validated screening tools and do not give a diagnosis.

Exercising safely

  • Talk to your GP before starting a new exercise program, particularly if you have a heart, blood pressure, breathing or joint condition. If you have a heart, kidney or fluid-balance condition, follow your clinician’s instructions on fluids and exercise.
  • Stop and seek medical advice if you feel chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness during exercise.
  • See your GP soon if anyone has seen you stop breathing, choke or gasp in your sleep, or if you feel drowsy while driving. Do not drive when drowsy.

Tester limitations

  • Early version. The tester is offered free so that men can try the program and give feedback. It may change, and features may be added or removed.
  • Runs in a browser. It opens in your phone’s web browser and is not an app-store app.
  • Manual entry. Every number is entered by hand. The tester does not record snoring itself and does not connect automatically to CPAP machines, watches or health apps. A built-in recorder and automatic sync are planned but are not available.
  • Separate snore app. Your snore score comes from a snore-recording app made by another company.
  • Separate breathing trainer. You need to buy your own handheld breathing trainer. Breathe Strong does not make or sell one.
  • Back-ups are up to you. In phone-only mode your logs exist only on that device, so back them up regularly.
  • Pricing. Pricing and availability of the full program have not been announced.

About Breathe Strong

Breathe Strong is an independently developed 52-week program for men over 40 who snore, live with obstructive sleep apnoea or use CPAP. It is currently in its tester stage.

It is developed by Craig, a medical scientist of 27 years based in Australia, who has trained in martial arts since the age of 12 and in strength training for more than 30 years. He is not a sleep physician. He built the program from published research and his own training experience, and he is following it himself. His story is on the home page.
